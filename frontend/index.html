How Orbit Solves The Defaulter Problem

1. Collateral Stake: Every member stakes 1 round of collateral upon joining.
2. Reputation-Sorted Turn: High-reputation members get earlier payouts; unproven members get later slots.
3. Slashing on Default: If a member misses their turn deadline, the contract seizes their collateral to fund the pot and slashes their reputation score (-150 pts).
