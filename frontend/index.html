How Orbit Solves The ROSCA Defaulter & Ghosting Problem

1. Full Anti-Ghosting Collateral (N-1)×C: Every member stakes (total_members - 1) × contribution upon joining, ensuring even the Round 1 recipient has profit ≤ 0 if they take the pot and ghost.
2. Defaulter Payout Skipping: claim_payout skips any member in defaulted_members and routes the pot strictly to honest members.
3. Zero Locked Funds (cancel_pending_circle & withdraw_on_termination): Members can cancel unstarted Pending circles or terminated Active circles to recover 100% of their collateral and deposits.
